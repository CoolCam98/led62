About Us

LED62 is a small business in Plant City, Florida that turns photos, logos, and original artwork into custom acrylic LED night lights. We design, laser-engrave, and assemble every Lumi light in-house, and you'll find us selling them at comic conventions across Florida and Georgia.

Every order is handled by the same small team from start to finish. We review your file, engrave it, check the finished light, and pack it for shipping ourselves.

Made In-House

Every light is cut and laser-engraved in our Plant City, FL shop and inspected before it ships.

Unique & Personal

Your light is made for you and nobody else. Whether it's a family photo, a sports logo, or a character you love — we bring it to life in glowing acrylic.

Customer First

If there's ever a problem with your light, email us and we'll work it out with you.
